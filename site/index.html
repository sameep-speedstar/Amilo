Why it works

The rules it lives by

An assistant, on purpose. Not a companion.

01Nothing happens without your OK

Amilo proposes; you confirm. It never touches your calendar or your accounts without an explicit yes, and every action is logged where you can see it.

02It reads your calendar, not your mood

No sentiment tracking, no emotional profiling, no streaks, no "talk to me" nudges. The best conversation ends quickly with your day improved.

03Memory you can inspect

Ask "what do you know about me" and get the full list — every routine and fact, with its source. Say "forget that" and it's gone. Nothing is remembered without asking first.

04It closes the loops it opens

"Did the Sharma call happen?" One follow-up in the evening, one the next morning, then it stops. Persistence with restraint — Amilo never nags.
