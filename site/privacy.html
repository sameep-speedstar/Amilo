Privacy policy

Effective 17 July 2026 · Amilo is operated by Speedstar AI Labs Private Limited, Bengaluru, India

Amilo is a personal assistant that works over WhatsApp and Telegram. Its job is to protect your attention — which only works if you can trust it completely with your information. This policy says, in plain language, what Amilo accesses, what it keeps, and how you stay in control. It is written to be read.

What Amilo accesses, and why

  • Google Calendar (the accounts you connect): Amilo reads your events to build briefings and detect conflicts, and can create or move events — only after you explicitly confirm each change. Every write is logged.
  • Gmail (the accounts you connect): Amilo reads message headers and short snippets to find what needs your attention — senders, subjects, deadlines, amounts due. It can archive a message only when you ask it to and confirm. Amilo never sends email on your behalf and never permanently deletes anything.
  • Messages you send Amilo: your texts and voice notes, used to carry out what you asked. Voice recordings are deleted immediately after transcription.
  • Notes, reminders, and things you ask Amilo to remember: stored only after you confirm, listed in full when you ask "what do you know about me", and deleted when you say "forget that".

What Amilo stores

As little as the job requires. Amilo keeps email headers and snippets — not full mailboxes — plus your calendar events, notes, reminders, and preferences. Connection tokens for your Google accounts are encrypted at rest. Your data is isolated to your account; no other user of Amilo can ever see it.

How Amilo uses AI

Amilo uses AI models (Anthropic's Claude, served through Google Cloud) to prioritise items, understand your requests, and write your briefings, and speech-recognition services to transcribe voice notes. These providers process data to provide the service to you; your data is not used to train their models and is never sold to anyone.

Google user data — Limited Use. Amilo's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Google user data is used only to provide Amilo's user-facing features, is never sold, is never used for advertising, and is never used to train generalised AI or machine-learning models.

What Amilo never does

  • No advertising, no selling of data, no sharing with data brokers — ever.
  • No action on your accounts without your explicit confirmation.
  • No mood tracking, sentiment analysis, or emotional profiling of you.
  • No reading of anything you haven't connected.

Your controls

These work as plain messages to Amilo, on any channel, at any time:

  • "pause" — Amilo stops everything and touches nothing until you say "resume".
  • "what do you know about me" — full list of everything remembered, with sources.
  • "forget <anything>" — removes that item.
  • "disconnect <account>" — removes that Google account's connection and its data.
  • "delete my data" — deletes your events, notes, reminders, messages and briefing history, and revokes Amilo's access to your Google accounts, within 24 hours. Amilo confirms when it's done.

Retention and security

Data is kept only while your account is active, and deleted per the commands above. All traffic is encrypted in transit; tokens are encrypted at rest; inbound messages from WhatsApp and Telegram are signature-verified. Amilo runs on Google Cloud infrastructure. Access by our team is restricted to operational metrics; your content is accessed only for debugging, only with your explicit permission, and every such access is logged.

Invite requests

If you ask for an invite on our website, we collect your name, phone number, email address, and how you heard about Amilo. We use these only to contact you about beta access, we never share or sell them, and we delete them on request — write to sales@speedstar.ai.

Website analytics

This website (the pages you are reading now) uses Google Analytics to understand aggregate traffic — which pages are visited and how visitors arrive. This applies to the website only. It never touches your conversations with Amilo, your connected accounts, or anything described above. You can block analytics with standard browser tools and the site works fully without it.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes materially, Amilo will tell you directly on your channel before the change takes effect. Questions, concerns, or grievances: sales@speedstar.ai. We reply as people, not as a form letter.
